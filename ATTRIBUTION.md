# Attribution

- **Positions**: aisstream.io (https://aisstream.io), a free AIS stream with a key, or a receiver of your own running AIS-catcher (https://github.com/jvde-github/AIS-catcher), read at /api/ships.json.
- **Photographs**: Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org), the category each IMO number has; and, with a key of the user's, Flickr (https://www.flickr.com) under the Creative Commons and public-domain licences that allow derivative works. Each photograph is credited to its photographer, source and licence on the page.
- **Weather** for the sea as it is now: Open-Meteo (https://open-meteo.com), no key.
- **Paintings**: fal.ai models, named on the page. The painting is made from the photograph (or the vessel's particulars) and the style words; the prompts are in `underway_core/lib/paint.py`.
- **Fonts**: IM Fell English and IM Fell English SC (Igino Marini, SIL Open Font License), Archivo Narrow (Omnibus-Type, SIL Open Font License); licences in `underway_core/lib/fonts/`.
- **The packer** (`sheet.js`, `pack.js`, `cut.js`) is shared with the Specimen Sheet and Overhead widgets.
